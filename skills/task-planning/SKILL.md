id: task-planning
version: 1.0.0
name: Task planning
description: Maintain a small explicit plan for multi-step work.
requiredTools:
  - plan_update
  - plan_mark
---
For multi-step work, publish a concise plan with one in_progress step at a time. Once it exists, mark progress with plan_mark in the same step as the work, and revise the plan only when the approach changes. The plan closes with the final answer. Do not plan simple one-step answers.
